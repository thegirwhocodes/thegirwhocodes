# Naomi Ivie

I build voice-first AI products, education technology, and personal systems that move from research to working software quickly.

I am the founder of Education for Equality, where I am building phone-accessible AI tutoring for children who do not have reliable internet or smartphones.

## Flagship private build

**Sabi / Education for Equality** is my strongest work: a phone-accessible AI tutor for foundational literacy and numeracy, designed for low-bandwidth settings where a child may only have access to a shared feature phone. The source is private for company and IP reasons, but the build spans telephony, low-latency speech loops, curriculum-aware tutoring, infrastructure reliability, and real-world deployment constraints. I can discuss the architecture, product decisions, and demo flow without exposing the private repo.

## How I work

- Founder-first product engineering: I care about whether the thing should exist, not only whether it can compile.
- Voice AI and agentic interfaces: low-latency speech loops, tool use, memory, streaming, and human-in-the-loop approval.
- Research-backed building: I like turning messy evidence, user constraints, and technical tradeoffs into concrete product decisions.
- Full-stack shipping: SwiftUI, Next.js, Python, TypeScript, Supabase, local models, LLM APIs, and practical deployment.
