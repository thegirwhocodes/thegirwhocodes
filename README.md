# Naomi Ivie

I build voice-first AI products, education technology, and personal systems that move from research to working software quickly.

I am the founder of Education for Equality, where I am building phone-accessible AI tutoring for children who do not have reliable internet or smartphones. The core Sabi infrastructure is private, but the work shapes how I build: low-latency voice, resilient systems, curriculum-aware AI, and products that have to work outside ideal conditions.

## How I work

- Founder-first product engineering: I care about whether the thing should exist, not only whether it can compile.
- Voice AI and agentic interfaces: low-latency speech loops, tool use, memory, streaming, and human-in-the-loop approval.
- Research-backed building: I like turning messy evidence, user constraints, and technical tradeoffs into concrete product decisions.
- Full-stack shipping: SwiftUI, Next.js, Python, TypeScript, Supabase, local models, LLM APIs, and practical deployment.
